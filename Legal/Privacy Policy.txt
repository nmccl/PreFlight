PreFlight — Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 23, 2026

PreFlight is a macOS developer tool that analyzes your Xcode projects for
App Store review readiness. It was built to work entirely on your Mac.
This policy explains what information PreFlight handles, where it lives,
and what never leaves your computer.


The Short Version

PreFlight does not collect, sell, or share your personal information. It
has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no servers
of our own. Everything the app knows about you and your projects stays on
your Mac, with one exception you control: if you add an App Store Connect
API key, PreFlight talks directly to Apple on your behalf.


Information Stored on Your Mac

PreFlight keeps a small amount of data locally so the app is useful
between launches. None of it is transmitted to us:

  - Settings. Your preferences (appearance, whether Apple Intelligence
    summaries are enabled, onboarding state) are stored in the app's
    preferences on your Mac.

  - Recent projects. PreFlight remembers the projects you've opened,
    using macOS security-scoped bookmarks so it can reopen them with your
    permission. You can remove entries at any time from the Home screen.

  - Reports. The most recent analysis report for each project is saved in
    the app's local container so you can revisit it. Reports never leave
    your Mac.

  - App Store Connect credentials. If you connect App Store Connect, your
    Issuer ID and Key ID are stored in the app's preferences, and your
    private key (.p8) is stored in the macOS Keychain on your Mac. Your
    credentials are never sent to us — we never see them.


Your Source Code

PreFlight reads your project's files locally in order to analyze them.
Your source code is never uploaded anywhere, never used for anything other
than producing your report, and never retained beyond what is described
above.


Information Sent Over the Network

  - App Store Connect (optional, at your direction). If you add an API
    key, PreFlight calls Apple's App Store Connect API directly
    (api.appstoreconnect.apple.com) using your credentials, solely to read
    your app's metadata — things like its privacy policy URL, description,
    screenshots, and subscription configuration. PreFlight only reads;
    it never modifies your apps. This traffic goes to Apple, not to us,
    and is subject to Apple's own privacy policy and terms.

  - Nothing else. PreFlight sends no telemetry, no crash reports, no
    usage statistics, and no identifiers to us or to any third party.


Apple Intelligence

If you enable AI summaries, they are generated by Apple Intelligence
on-device. Your report data is processed locally by the system model and
is not sent to us.


Children

PreFlight is a professional developer tool and is not directed at
children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from
anyone, including children.


Your Choices

  - You can remove recent projects from the Home screen at any time.
  - You can remove your App Store Connect key in Settings, which deletes
    the private key from your Keychain.
  - You can delete the app, which removes its local container, including
    settings and saved reports.


Changes to This Policy

If PreFlight's privacy practices ever change — for example, if a future
version adds optional crash reporting — we will update this policy and
its effective date before the change takes effect.
